How long will this patient stay in hospital?

Enter findings at presentation. The model estimates the chance of being discharged alive by each day, treating in-hospital death as a competing outcome. It was trained on — patients with TBI from India, Jordan, Florida and California.

Research use only. Not validated for clinical decisions. Estimates come from four urban cohorts and can be wrong for an individual patient by days to weeks, especially for long intensive-care stays. Everything runs in your browser; nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

How well it works

Five-fold cross-validation on the pooled data. C-index measures how well the model ranks who goes home sooner (0.5 is chance). Brier score measures the accuracy of the predicted probabilities (lower is better). The reference for typical stay is simply predicting the median stay for everyone.
