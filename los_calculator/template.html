How long will this patient stay in hospital?

Enter findings at presentation. The tool estimates the risk of dying in hospital and, for patients who go home, the likely length of stay. It was trained on — patients with TBI from India, Jordan and California (USA).

Research use only. Not validated for clinical decisions. Everything runs in your browser; nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

How well it works

Five-fold cross-validation on all — pooled patients: each patient's estimate comes from a model that never saw that patient.

How the model was chosen

Five approaches were compared on the same folds. The highlighted one had the smallest error in predicted stay and a range that holds 80% of stays as intended, with mortality discrimination equal to the others.

ApproachMortality AUROCPredicted / observed deathsStay error (days)80% range holdsRange width (days)
Death risk: gradient-boosted classifier. Length of stay: gradient-boosted quantile trees on log length of stay among patients discharged alive, with a split-conformal correction so the 80% range holds its stated coverage. Trained on the TITCO registry (India), a pediatric trauma dataset (Jordan) and an adult TBI dataset (California, USA). Code: github.com/shrinitbabel/TBI-transfer-learning, branch los-calculator. Side project; not part of the published study.
